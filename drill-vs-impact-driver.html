Drill vs Impact Driver for Homeowners (Which One Do You Need?)

If you are a homeowner trying to decide between a drill and an impact driver, the simplest answer is this: most people should start with a cordless drill, then add an impact driver later if projects get bigger.

Quick Answer

  • If you only buy one tool: get a cordless drill/driver. It handles most normal homeowner tasks.
  • If you start doing bigger projects: add an impact driver for driving long screws and tougher fastening jobs.
  • If you want the easiest setup: buy a drill + impact driver combo kit.

What a Drill Is Best For

A drill is the most versatile first power tool for homeowners. It is the right tool for drilling holes and handling normal screw-driving jobs around the house.

  • Hanging shelves and curtain rods
  • Assembling furniture
  • Installing cabinet hardware
  • Drilling into wood, drywall, and light metal
  • General repairs and small projects

If you only buy one power tool first, it should usually be a cordless drill/driver.

What an Impact Driver Is Best For

An impact driver is designed to drive screws and fasteners with more force and less bogging down. It becomes useful once you move beyond light homeowner tasks and start tackling tougher jobs.

  • Driving long deck screws
  • Fastening large hardware
  • Building shelves or framing projects
  • Driving repeated screws faster with less wrist strain

A compact impact driver makes bigger DIY projects much easier once your workload grows.

The Simplest Buying Plan for Homeowners

  1. Start with a cordless drill if you are buying your first tool.
  2. Add an impact driver later if you start driving lots of long screws.
  3. If budget allows, buy a combo kit from the start so you get both tools, batteries, and charger in one purchase.

Common Buying Mistakes

  • Buying the cheapest no-name kit with weak batteries
  • Ignoring battery platform when choosing a brand
  • Buying huge tools that feel awkward in tight spaces
  • Thinking an impact driver replaces a drill for all jobs

Which One Should You Buy First?

For most homeowners, the answer is still simple: buy the drill first. It handles the widest range of common home tasks. The impact driver becomes the second tool once you are taking on more demanding projects.
