What an Impact Driver Is For (In Plain English)

An impact driver is built for driving screws and fasteners fast and clean—especially long screws into wood—without bogging down like many drills do.

What Specs Matter (Beginner-Friendly)

  • Size (head length): shorter is easier for homeowner work.
  • Speed settings: helps avoid stripping screws.
  • Battery platform: choose the brand you’ll expand into later.
  • Bit changes: quick-change collet saves time.

Do You Still Need a Drill?

Yes. A drill is still the best tool for drilling holes. An impact driver is your “fastener specialist.” Many homeowners eventually own both.

  • Buying an impact driver with a random battery system you’ll never expand.
