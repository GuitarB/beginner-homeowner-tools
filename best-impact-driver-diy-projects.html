What an Impact Driver Is Actually For

An impact driver is built for driving screws and fasteners with more force and less strain than a normal drill. It shines when you are building, repairing, or fastening repeatedly.

What Specs Matter Most

  • Size: compact models are easier for homeowner use
  • Battery platform: pick a brand you may expand later
  • Speed settings: helps control smaller fasteners better
  • Quick-change collet: makes bit changes faster and easier

Do You Still Need a Drill?

Yes. An impact driver is not a replacement for a drill. A drill is still better for drilling holes and more general-purpose homeowner work.

Common Buying Mistakes

  • Buying an impact driver first when a drill would cover more jobs
  • Choosing a random battery system you will not expand
  • Buying the cheapest kit with weak batteries
  • Ignoring size when most homeowner jobs happen indoors or in tight spots
