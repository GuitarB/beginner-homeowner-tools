What Specs Actually Matter (Don’t Overthink This)

  • Battery platform: Pick a brand you’ll want to expand later (lights, impact driver, saw).
  • Size/weight: Compact beats “more power” for most homeowners.
  • Clutch settings: Helps avoid stripping screws.
  • 2-speed gearbox: One speed for drilling, one for driving screws.
  • Chuck: 1/2-inch is common and versatile.

Do You Need an Impact Driver Too?

If you’re mostly hanging shelves, assembling furniture, and doing small repairs, a drill is enough. If you’re driving lots of long screws (decks, framing, big projects), add an impact driver later.

Common Buying Mistakes

  • Buying the cheapest kit with weak batteries and no ecosystem.
  • Buying a huge “pro” drill that’s annoying to use in tight spaces.
  • Ignoring battery Ah (runtime) if you do longer projects.
